**Quant read:**
- A headline/core gap of ~0.9pp driven by energy + freight = **cost-push / shipping-lane inflation, not demand inflation.** The Fed's framework reads *core* (services + demand) → **core ~2.5% = no hiking urgency**; energy/goods is treated as transient/supply-side.
- **Rates/duration:** hot headline keeps the front-end from pricing aggressive cuts and grinds **long duration (TLT) lower via term premium → curve steepens (bear steepener).** Long-end is the marginal loser; the belly (IEF) is relatively cushioned.
- **Equities:** this is a **rotation-agnostic tailwind for energy/producers/shipping** (XLE/XOM/CVX, ZIM) and a **headwind for long-duration/growth** on the term-premium grind — but the tame *core* caps the damage to broad tech, which is why SPY can sit at all-time highs despite a hot headline.

**Actionable watch:**
- **Fade long-duration (TLT); prefer belly (IEF) over the 20-yr+ end** while headline stays hot.
- Long **energy (XLE/XOM/CVX) + freight (ZIM)** as the direct expression of the "shipping-lane" repricing.
- If a subsequent print shows **core re-accelerating toward ~3%**, the "not a demand story" framing breaks → that's the real risk trigger (hike repricing, broad equity de-rate). Watch core, not headline.
