## 2. Business overview & price drivers

TGB is a two-mine copper platform executing a genuine growth transition:

- **Gibraltar (producing, the cash engine):** ~110–115M lbs Cu guided for 2026 at a low C1 of ~**$2.41/lb** (Q2'26), plus a molybdenum by-product credit (~$0.65/lb). Strong but mature; grades ~0.25%, recoveries ~82%. 2025 full-year: 98.1M lbs at C1 $2.66.
- **Florence Copper (the growth kicker):** Arizona in-situ SX/EW project, first-ever new US copper cathode supply in a decade. Built on time/budget ($275M). First cathode Feb 2026; **5.2M lbs produced in Q2'26** (first full quarter) at C1 ~$4.72/lb. **2026 target 30–35M lbs**, rising toward ~85M lbs/yr at full ramp — this roughly doubles the portfolio's volume. High current cost, but it's a ramp-up asset and scales down with volume.
- **Development pipeline:** Yellowhead (25-yr mine life, 178M lbs/yr at C1 ~$1.90, NPV $2.0B @ $4.25 Cu) — environmental assessment started. New Prosperity — $75M received from BC province.

**What drives the price (in order of importance):**
1. **The copper price** — the dominant swing factor. Realized $6.10/lb in Q2'26; spot ~$6.58/lb on 8/14/26 (all-time high ~$6.83 in Aug 2026). Management explicitly says results are "significantly leveraged to copper price."
2. **Florence ramp execution** — wellfield expansion, solution flow rates (3,182 gpm Q2), PLS grades (1.6 g/L). Every month of ramp beats or misses.
3. **Production/guidance delivery at Gibraltar** and cost control (diesel inflation +$0.10–0.15/lb 2026).
4. **Sentiment/momentum** — the stock is up **+159% over 1 year** and +51% YTD, high-beta, news-reactive.

**Recent catalyst — Q2 2026 (reported Aug 5, 2026):** Revenue $331M, **Adjusted EBITDA $125M** (record), **operating cash flow $183M**, Adj net income $40.5M ($0.11/sh), consolidated production **36M lbs (+80% YoY)**. ## 4. Far-horizon risks — AGAINST

The thesis is far from risk-free, and the negatives are concentrated:

- **Valuation is no longer cheap.** The 159% 1-yr run has front-loaded a lot. Trailing P/E ~408; **EV/EBITDA ~19.3**; P/S 4.4; **P/B 5.2**. Levered FCF is **negative (−$104M ttm)** — the company is spending heavily (Florence capex + wellfield) and earnings per share have barely caught up. You're paying a growth multiple before growth is fully in the numbers.
- **High beta / momentum unwind risk.** Beta ~2.0, up 159% in a year. If copper pulls back (it's at an all-time high — mean reversion is a real tail), TGB is a 2x beta way to give it back. Event reactions in the data are choppy (several "positive" updates met with negative next-day moves).
- **Dilution history / financing gap.** The company has done repeated equity raises (Nov'25, Feb'26 + the $172.8M Oct'25 raise at $4.05). With FCF negative and development still requiring capital, further dilution is a live risk — the ~366M share count keeps creeping up, diluting EPS.
- **Florence ramp risk.** Current C1 ~$4.72/lb is high; it only becomes attractive if it scales. Wellfield expansion, solution flows, PLS grades, groundwater/regulatory (in-situ acid injection in Arizona) are all execution/permitting risks. A hiccup is directly priced into a stock trading at this multiple.
- **Commodity single-point concentration.** ~100% of revenue is copper (+moly). No diversification; earnings are a pure function of one metal's price.
- **Cost inflation.** Diesel +$0.10–0.15/lb at Gibraltar in 2026 (partly offset by moly credits). Acid, explosives, and maintenance costs all rising.
- **Weak absolute profitability.** TTM net income only ~$16M on $985M revenue (1.6% margin). The "record" cash flow is EBITDA/operating cash-flow driven, not bottom-line earnings yet.
