## Method and caveats

- Source: `/home/bottl/Financial_Development/tradingview_watchlists.json`; 57 symbols total: 5 Small AI and 52 Minerals & Uranium.
- ThetaData supplied 269 daily OHLCV rows for most successes. The response did not expose a usable date field in the parsed rows, so the report labels the run by requested window and fetched spot/last-close values rather than pretending to know a per-row last date.
- EMA values use standard recursive EMA spans 20, 50, and 200. RSI is a 14-period close-based reading. Returns use the last available close versus approximately 30 and 90 observations; max drawdown uses the trailing 90 observations. Average dollar volume is mean(close × volume) over the last 30 observations.
- ATM IV is a live options-chain snapshot only for the four shortlisted names, using the nearest valid future expiry near 30 days and the nearest valid call strike. IV is context/risk sizing only; options are not recommended or traded.
- Fundamental checks are sanity checks from company IR releases/search results, not a full valuation or audited forensic review. Recheck the quote and news immediately before placing any order.
